Let's Hang

Plan hangs right in your group chat. iPhone, works with Messages.

Let's Hang turns "we should meet up" into a plan. Pick a place and a time and send it to your group chat as a card everyone can tap. Friends vote, say they're coming, and see who's on the way, all inside Messages. There's no account and no server: your groups stay on your phone and the plans live in your chats.

  • Hang cards in Messages: a place and a time as a card the whole group can tap and update.
  • Vote on anything: the place, the time, or a question like "Which movie?". Friends can nominate places too.
  • Who's here: see who's on the way and who has arrived, with one-tap "running late" updates.
  • Distance, directions and reminders: travel time on foot, by car or by bus, plus a leave-now reminder.
  • Every kind of hang: friends, family events and holidays, birthdays and surprise parties, date nights, book clubs, movie nights, after work, playdates and more.
  • Ideas when you're stuck: Surprise us picks a place nearby; Meet in the middle is fair for everyone.
  • Your style: Classic, Cute or Bold themes with matching icons, and fun avatars.
  • Private by design: no account, no tracking, and location shown only to your group, only during the hang.

SLT Board

A friendly "Now and Next" picture board for therapy sessions and daily routines.

SLT Board helps children follow a session one step at a time. Each step is a picture card: what we're doing now, and what comes next. When a step is done, the child taps "I finished!", gets a cheer and a short play break, and the next card moves up. It was made for speech and language therapy and works just as well for morning routines. Everything stays on the phone.

  • Now and Next board: a big, bright Now card with a bouncing pointer, and the next steps lined up below. Up to 10 steps per session.
  • Picture cards: around 50 ready-made cartoon cards, or take your own photos. Quick one-time photos are kept only if you choose.
  • Sessions you can reuse: save sessions, see the last one and the most used at a glance, or start a quick "Snap session".
  • Rewards that motivate: a different celebration after every step, tappable play-time toys with fun sounds, and a sticker for each step at the end.
  • Calm while working: gentle background sounds (rain, waves, a stream or wind) to help concentration, and upbeat music only for rewards.
  • A voice that feels personal: use a natural iPhone voice, your own Personal Voice, or record your own short cheers.
  • Private: no account and no internet. Photos and recordings never leave the phone and are left out of backups.

Bus Home

Three buttons, one answer: which bus to catch, and when to leave.

Bus Home is a tiny, fully offline bus planner. Tap where you want to go and it uses your location and the bus timetable on the phone to tell you when to leave, which bus to take, and when you'll arrive, including the walk at each end. It needs no signal, no account and no data plan.

  • Three big buttons: your everyday destinations, one tap each.
  • Leave-by time: when to set off, the bus to catch, the stop to walk to, and your arrival time.
  • Works offline: the timetable is on the phone and location comes from GPS only.
  • Timetable changes built in: switches to the new schedule on the day it starts.
  • Where am I?: shows your position and the nearest stop.
  • Private: no account, no tracking, nothing sent anywhere.

ShoulderCheck

On-device camera awareness on the go, with entertainment built in.

ShoulderCheck is an iPhone idea for when you're riding a bus or a cab. It keeps an on-device camera check running and gives you something to enjoy at the same time. iPhone stops an app's camera as soon as you switch apps, so ShoulderCheck brings the entertainment inside its own screen instead: music, podcasts, radio, video and quick games.

  • Camera that keeps running: on-device detection, nothing uploaded.
  • Entertainment inside the app: music, podcasts, radio, video and quick games without leaving it.
  • Status: early prototype, paused while Let's Hang comes first.

All MyI apps share the same idea: no accounts, no tracking, and your data stays on your phone. Only Let's Hang is planned for the App Store; the others are private or in development.
