Let's Hang FAQ

How do I start a hang?

Tap Start a hang in the app, pick a group (or choose the chat in Messages), the kind of hang, a place and a time, then Send. In Messages you can also tap +, choose Let's Hang, then New hang.

Do my friends need the app?

To vote or update a card in Messages, yes: they need Let's Hang on their iPhone. Friends without it see the card's picture and text. A preview page for friends without the app is on the way.

Does it work on Android?

Not yet. Cards are an iMessage feature.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Hang details travel inside the cards in your Messages chats. Let's Hang has no server and no account.

Does Let's Hang track my location?

No. Your location is used on your phone, while you use the app, to show distances and travel times. It isn't saved or sent anywhere.

Can I hide my location?

Yes. On the hang card, tap Hide my location: free for an hour at a time, longer with Plus. Show it again any time, free and instant.

How does surprise mode work?

Plan the real hang in the organisers' chat with "It's a surprise" on. In the guest of honour's chat, send a Surprise teaser that shows only what you choose.

Who can use Let's Hang?

People aged 13 and over.

Does it cost anything?

Let's Hang is free. An optional Plus subscription for organisers is planned; friends you invite never pay.

How do I delete everything?

In the app: Your data and privacy, then Delete everything. Cards already sent stay in your chats; delete them in Messages.
