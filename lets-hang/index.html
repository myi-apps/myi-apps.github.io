Let's Hang

Plan hangs right in your group chat. Pick a place and a time, let everyone vote, and see who's on the way. No account, no server: your plans stay with you and your friends.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone. Works with Messages.

How it works

1. Start a hang

In the app or in Messages: pick the kind of hang, a place and a time.

2. Friends vote and reply

Everyone taps the card to vote, say they're coming, or tell you they're running late.

3. Get there together

See who's here, get directions and reminders, and celebrate with a "We made it!" card.

Private by design

Let's Hang has no account and no server. Groups and settings stay on your iPhone. Hang details travel inside the cards in your Messages chats. Your location is used on your phone to show distances and is never stored or sent by us. Read the privacy policy.
