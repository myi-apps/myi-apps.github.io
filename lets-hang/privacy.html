Who we are

Let's Hang is an app by myI Digital Services (MyI) ([address to add]; no company registered yet). Contact: see the Support page.

The short version

  • Let's Hang has no server and no account. We don't collect, see or sell your data.
  • What the app stores (your groups, the people in them, their usual areas) stays on your iPhone.
  • Hang cards are sent in your Messages chats through Apple's iMessage, like any message you send.
  • Your location is used on your iPhone to show distances and travel times. Let's Hang doesn't store it or send it anywhere.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time: Let's Hang › Your data and privacy.

What the app handles

| Data | Why | Where it lives | How long |

|---|---|---|---|

| Groups you create (name, type) | To plan hangs with the same people | Your iPhone only | Until you delete it |

| Members you add from Contacts (name, phone or email) | To message or call the group | Your iPhone only | Until you delete it |

| Usual areas you set for members | To suggest fair places to meet | Your iPhone only | Until you delete it |

| Hang details (place, time, votes, running late) | So the group can see them | Inside the cards in your Messages chats | As long as the chat keeps them |

| Your current location (while using the app) | Distance, travel times, directions | Used on your iPhone, not stored | Not kept |

We pick contacts through Apple's contact picker, so Let's Hang only sees the people you choose, not your address book.

Surprise mode

A surprise teaser only contains what the organisers switch on. Hidden details are not in the card at all.

Other companies

  • Apple delivers Messages, Maps and FaceTime under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Google Maps opens only if you choose it for directions.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking companies.

Children

Let's Hang is for people aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly allow children under 13.

Your rights (EU/UK GDPR and similar laws)

You can see, export and delete your data yourself in the app. Because we don't hold your data on a server, there's nothing for us to hand over or erase on our side. You can also contact us through the Support page or complain to your data protection authority (in Ireland: the Data Protection Commission).

Future features

Planned features such as the "on the way" live location and sharing groups through iCloud will be described here before they launch.
