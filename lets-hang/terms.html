Using Let's Hang

Let's Hang helps you plan meetups with people you already know. You must be 13 or older. Use it kindly and lawfully: no harassment, spam, or content that harms others.

Your content

What you write on cards (places, notes, questions) is sent through Messages to the people you choose. You're responsible for it. We don't host it on a server.

Safety

Plans and locations are only as reliable as the information you and your friends share. Don't use the app while driving. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Subscriptions

An optional Plus subscription may be offered through the App Store, with its price, period and renewal terms shown before you buy. You can cancel in your Apple account settings.
